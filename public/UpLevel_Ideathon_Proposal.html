06. The Voice Subsystem & Persona Intelligence

The voice system is a major technological differentiator for UpLevel. Instead of generic text-to-speech, it features an intelligent multi-tiered audio engine:

1. Priority Queue & Event Triggers

Voice lines trigger on 18 distinct events (`task_complete`, `level_up`, `gym_finish`, `budget_exceeded`, `focus_end`, `streak_milestone`). Important events (rank up) interrupt lower-priority idle lines. An 8-second cooldown prevents audio fatigue.

2. Dual-Engine Audio Routing

The system inspects `customAudio` mapping first. If an audio file (such as the coach motivational speech) is present, it plays via HTML5 Audio with instant visual speech-bubble captions. Otherwise, it adapts browser Web Speech synthesis with custom pitch, rate, and timbre.

11. Ethical Safeguards & Well-Being Protection

Unlike predatory social media apps designed to induce addiction, UpLevel implements strict well-being guardrails:

Anti-Burnout Rest Protection

The streak engine includes "Rest Day Passes". If a student studies 6 days straight, taking Sunday off does not wipe their streak. UpLevel rewards sustainable pacing over toxic hustle culture.

Positive Character Coaching Guidelines

Character voice lines never attack self-worth or use abusive language. Even stern characters like Madara Uchiha deliver constructive discipline focused entirely on habits, growth, and standing back up.

Audio Fatigue & Quiet Hours

Built-in voice cooldowns (minimum 8s between voice prompts), a max cap of 3 idle prompts/hour, and scheduled Quiet Hours ensure the audio coach never interrupts sleep or quiet libraries.

Absolute Data Privacy

All budget figures, personal habits, notes, and study quizzes stay strictly in the browser's local sandbox. No sensitive financial or health data is harvested or transmitted to third parties.
